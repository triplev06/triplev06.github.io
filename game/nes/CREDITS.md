## Layout and progression reference

https://www.zeldadungeon.net/the-legend-of-zelda-walkthrough/level-1-the-eagle/

The route is H8, I8, I7, I6, I5, I4, H4. The Eagle uses the original room-map sheet and includes its bow cellar, boomerang, keys, map, compass, Aquamentus, heart container, and Triforce fragment. The minimap is available immediately as requested. Keyboard/touch input, local checkpoint saves, retry behavior, and optional synthesized sound effects are browser adaptations. Enemy timing is recreated, not emulated. Optional bomb shortcuts and off-route shops are outside this excerpt. The bow is collectible; arrows are not supplied because its shop is outside the selected route. The sword and boomerang complete the dungeon.


## Audio
The site owner supplied the original Overworld of Hyrule, Dungeon Theme, Small Item Get, Link Dies, and Triforce Shard Collected MP3s. Original game music and sounds belong to their respective rights holders. Additional combat feedback is synthesized. Sound starts on Begin / Continue, remembers the mute setting, and pauses with gameplay.
