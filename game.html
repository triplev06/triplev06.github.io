Enter / I opens the inventory. Use arrows to select a B item, then Enter to return. Hold a direction against a block to push it. Hold attack to keep swinging. Face your target. Sword beams fire at full life. Your shield blocks rocks when facing them. Touch controls appear on small screens.

The original enemies

Octoroks · Moblins · Keese · Stalfos · Gel · Goriyas · Wallmasters · Blade Traps · Aquamentus

Saved when you change rooms or collect treasure. Fall in battle? Retry from a safe entrance with your items.
